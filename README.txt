HOSTING
1. Extract saleem-website.zip.
2. Open the saleem-website folder.
3. Upload its contents to your domain's document root, usually public_html.
4. Keep index.html, styles.css, app.js, and the assets folder together.
5. Visit your domain to view the website.

No installation, build step, database, or server-side code is required.
The folder also works in a subfolder because asset paths are relative.
You can open index.html directly on your computer for a local preview.
